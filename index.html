    help

    what is this

    two things in one. cards is flashcards, audio records a chrome tab. swap with the big words at the top left.

    make a deck

    type a name in the bar, hit create. each deck shows up in the list below it with its own face.

    your decks

    click one to switch to it — the black one is the deck you're on. right-click any of them to rename, delete, or change look (eight shapes, and the one you pick travels with the deck's code). ctrl+z puts back whatever you just deleted. the smiley opens the same list as a dropdown, where you can drag to reorder.

    add cards

    the create tile takes you to a question / answer form. cards land in whichever deck is open.

    practice

    the four answers fill the page as quadrants, and the keyboard matches where they sit: qwert top-left, yuiop top-right, asdf/zxcv bottom-left, hjkl/nm bottom-right. wrong answer → click or press anything to move on.

    audio

    hit record and pick a chrome tab — tick "also share tab audio" or you get silence. it watches a small box over the speaker's name and starts a fresh clip whenever that name changes; the counter in the meter tells you how many switches it's caught. clips under 2s are binned. the gear shows the video so you can aim that box, the ↓ saves every clip as mp3 oldest-first, and the bin clears the lot.

    share a deck

    the square button next to create opens deck codes. copy gives you a code for the deck you're on — send it to anyone. paste one in and hit load to get their deck.

    don't lose your stuff

    everything lives in this browser only, so clearing site data wipes it. a code is also a backup — copy one somewhere safe.
